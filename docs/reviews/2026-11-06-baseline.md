# Tridoku product review: baseline

- **Date:** 6 Nov 2026 (puzzle #310)
- **Code:** `52cfb65`
- **Puzzles analysed:** 609 (21 Mar – 8 Nov 2026)
- **Backlog built from this review:** [`docs/backlog/`](../backlog/README.md)

**Method.** I read the full codebase and played complete puzzles in a headless browser at 1440px (Medium) and 390px (Easy). I also tested:
- a 360×640 phone screen
- four time zones
- a clock crossing midnight
- a missing puzzle date
- a failed puzzle download
- keyboard-only play

Every published puzzle was run through an independent solver and a difficulty grader based on solving techniques. No code was changed. The scripts that reproduce this are in [`scripts/eval/`](../../scripts/eval/README.md).

**Labels.** **[V]** means verified by running something. **[H]** means a hypothesis that needs player data. Screenshots are in [`2026-11-06-baseline/`](2026-11-06-baseline/).

---

## Executive summary

Tridoku is a free daily triangular Sudoku with three difficulties, a well-drawn SVG board and a pipeline that publishes new puzzles every day. Its biggest strength is the puzzles themselves: all 609 have exactly one solution, the givens are symmetric, and the rules modal teaches the key deduction well. Its biggest risk is that the retention loop is broken. Streaks reset to 1 on about 80% of days because of a date-format mismatch, the puzzle number differs by time zone, and the "Hard" label often contradicts how hard the puzzle actually is. A daily puzzle game whose streaks and labels can't be trusted will not keep players. The highest-leverage change is to fix the streak bug, then rank difficulty by the solving techniques a puzzle needs rather than by how many numbers it starts with.

## 1. Value proposition and positioning

**What's working:** The page metadata ("A daily triangular sudoku puzzle game") is clear (`app/layout.tsx:12`). Calling it "Sudoku with triangles" is the right hook.

**Issues**
- **1.1 High [V]:** The first screen (`desktop-01-first.png`) shows a triangle icon, "Welcome to Tridoku", "First time here?" and three colored buttons. It never says what Tridoku is, that it's daily, or that it's Sudoku-like. The board itself is never shown before you commit to a difficulty.
- **1.2 Medium [V]:** The difficulty cards describe themselves as "40–50 starting numbers" (`components/tridoku-game.tsx:223-255`). That's a measure from the generator's point of view and tells a new player nothing.
- **1.3 Medium [H]:** The target player isn't defined. The product reads as "for existing Sudoku fans", but the warm palette and three tiers suggest a casual daily habit. The difference matters for whether Easy should be the default.

**Recommendations**
- **1.a** Replace the welcome hero with a static, non-playable preview of today's board plus one line, e.g. *"Sudoku on a triangle. 81 cells, 4 rules, a new puzzle every day."*
- **1.b** Relabel the cards by experience, e.g. "Easy · ~5 min · great first puzzle", "Medium · needs one trick", "Hard · for Sudoku regulars". Fill in real median times once you're measuring them (see §10).
- **1.c** Make Easy the default for first-time visitors with a single "Play today's puzzle" button, and keep the tier picker for returning players.

## 2. First-time experience and onboarding

**What's working:** The rules modal (`components/rules-modal.tsx`) is the best thing in the product. Each rule gets its own diagram, the "No Touching" step shows edge contact, corner contact and a valid case (`desktop-03-rules-notouch.png`), and the "Power Cells" step teaches the actual aha deduction. It credits the original inventor. Time from choosing a difficulty to the first placed digit is about 65 ms, so there's no load friction.

**Issues**
- **2.1 High [V]:** The rules never open on their own. On a first visit, "How to Play" is an optional outline button (`tridoku-game.tsx:199`). A first-timer who taps "Easy" sees a multicolored triangle with no idea what the yellow, blue and green cells mean.
- **2.2 High [V]:** Cell colors carry rule meaning (outer edge vs inner triangle), but that meaning changes with difficulty. Blue means outer edge on Easy and inner triangle on Hard (`app/globals.css:52-63`). The rules modal teaches only the Easy colors.
- **2.3 Medium [V]:** The modal has 10 steps, and 6 of them are about controls or tips. **[H]** Many players will close it before the "Power Cells" step, which is the most valuable one.
- **2.4 Low [V]:** The no-touch rule is the one that's genuinely new compared with Sudoku, yet the game gives no visual cue for it when you select a cell.

**Recommendations**
- **2.a** On a first visit (no stats in localStorage), open a three-card version automatically: regions, edges plus inner triangle, no touching. Put the control tips in a "Tips" tab.
- **2.b** Show "Power Cells" as a contextual tip the first time the player sits idle for 60 seconds.
- **2.c** Fix the edge and inner-triangle colors across all difficulties, and signal difficulty with an accent.
- **2.d** When a cell is selected, lightly shade its 12 touching neighbors.

## 3. Core gameplay and puzzle design

**What's working [V]:** All 609 puzzles have exactly one solution (checked with an independent solver). The generator removes cells in 120°-rotated triplets, so every puzzle is symmetric (`scripts/generate-puzzles.mjs:612-679`). All Easy puzzles can be solved with simple logic.

**How hard the puzzles really are [V].** Each puzzle graded by the simplest set of techniques that solves it. Tier 1: singles only. Tier 2: adds "power cell" eliminations. Tier 3: adds naked pairs and triples. Tier 4+: needs something stronger, possibly guessing.

| Label | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4+ | Givens (actual) | Givens (UI says) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 184 | 19 | 0 | 0 | 42–48 | 40–50 |
| Medium | 102 | 86 | 1 | **14** | 33–42 | 30–40 |
| Hard | **34** | 120 | 4 | **45** | 27–36 (avg 31.8) | 24–30 |

**Issues**
- **3.1 Critical [V]:** The labels are unreliable. 17% of Hard puzzles need only singles, and 7% of Medium puzzles need techniques beyond pairs. On 6 Nov, Medium is Tier 4+ and Hard is Tier 1. 22% of Hard puzzles may need guessing. Difficulty is driven only by the number of givens (`generate-puzzles.mjs:174-177`).
- **3.2 High [V]:** The advertised ranges are wrong (see table). The generator accepts results up to 6 above target (`generate-puzzles.mjs:721`).
- **3.3 High [V]:** There's no undo. Placing a pen digit hides the cell's pencil marks, and the digit isn't removed from neighbors' marks (`hooks/use-tridoku.ts` `setValue`). There are no hints; a stuck player's only escape is Reset.
- **3.4 Medium [V]:** Error checking is off by default. If the board is full but invalid, nothing happens.
- **3.5 Low [V]:** The uniqueness check gives up after 1M iterations (`generate-puzzles.mjs:313`). If it gives up after finding one solution, it reports "unique". No current puzzle is affected.

**Recommendations**
- **3.a** Add the tier grader to `generate-puzzles.mjs`. Easy = Tier 1, Medium = Tier 2, Hard = Tier 2–3 with a minimum number of eliminations; reject Tier 4+. Store tier and technique counts in `metadata`.
- **3.b** Single-level undo with a button next to Reset and Ctrl/Cmd-Z.
- **3.c** When a digit is placed, remove it from the pencil marks of its touching cells and every region and edge it belongs to.
- **3.d** One hint type: "highlight a cell that can be solved now".
- **3.e** When the board is full but invalid, show a toast: "Something's off. Turn on error check?"

## 4. UX and interaction design

**What's working:** Number pad buttons are 56×56 px. Pen/Pencil switches with P or Space. Reset asks for confirmation. Progress is saved per difficulty and survives a reload **[V]**.

**Issues**
- **4.1 High [V]:** At 360×640 the number pad starts at y=690, below the fold (`mobile360-hard.png`). There's about 70 px of empty space between the timer row and the board.
- **4.2 High [V]:** On a 390 px screen a triangle is about 38×33 px; the largest circle inside is about 22 px, half Apple's 44 pt guideline. Hit areas are the polygons themselves (`tridoku-board.tsx:249`). **[H]** Expect mis-taps.
- **4.3 Medium [V]:** Reloading mid-puzzle drops you back on the welcome screen. The cards don't show "In progress".
- **4.4 Medium [V]:** Pencil marks go up to 6 (the comment says 3, `use-tridoku.ts:355`); at 4 or more the text is about 7 px on mobile (`mobile-04-midgame.png`).
- **4.5 Medium [V]:** Givens are bold and entries regular weight, both black (`tridoku-board.tsx:278-279`).
- **4.6 Low [V]:** The highlight-a-digit gesture is only discoverable through the rules. The number pad doesn't dim digits already fully placed.

**Recommendations**
- **4.a** Under 700 px tall: collapse the header, put timer and controls on the tab row, remove board padding. Target: board and pad fit in 640 px.
- **4.b** Snap taps to the nearest cell centroid.
- **4.c** Returning players skip the welcome screen; show "Continue · 04:12" on the card.
- **4.d** Cap corner pencil marks at 3 or use a 3×3 mini-grid with a minimum font size. Color player entries in teal.
- **4.e** Dim a number-pad digit once all 9 copies are placed.

## 5. Visual design and brand

**What's working:** Cohesive palette; crisp board at every size; light mode looks good (`desktop-10-stats.png` background).

**Issues**
- **5.1 High [V]:** Dark-mode region lines are grey `#7e7e7e` on light cells, 3.1:1 vs 16:1 in light mode (`globals.css:140`, `desktop-11-dark.png`).
- **5.2 Medium [V]:** The OG image is 1024×1024 (`layout.tsx:25`) declared as `summary_large_image` (2:1); it's cropped and doesn't show the board.
- **5.3 Low [V]:** No real logo; the header uses a generic icon-library triangle.
- **5.4 Low [V]:** A stray "0" renders in the stats modal when best time is 0 (`stats-modal.tsx:93`).

**Recommendations**
- **5.a** Keep region lines near-black in dark mode or darken the cell fills; check 4.5:1.
- **5.b** Daily 1200×630 OG image via `next/og` with a partly filled board.
- **5.c** A mark made of 9 small triangles; doubles as favicon and share motif.

## 6. Accessibility

**What's working:** Dialogs move focus correctly; buttons have `aria-label`s.

**Issues**
- **6.1 Critical [V]:** Cells are `<g onClick>` with no `tabindex`, role or label (`tridoku-board.tsx:249`). Tab skips the board; arrows need an existing selection. Keyboard-only players can't place a digit.
- **6.2 High [V]:** The SVG has no role or label.
- **6.3 High [V]:** Errors are signalled by color alone: 1.49:1 on Hard's pink edges, 2.39:1 on Medium's pink inner cells. The error overlay is skipped on the selected cell.
- **6.4 Medium [V]:** The selection outline animates forever even with reduced motion requested.
- **6.5 Medium [V]:** Welcome card subtitles: Hard 2.06:1, Medium 3.19:1.

**Recommendations**
- **6.a** `role="grid"` board, `gridcell`s, roving tabindex, first empty cell selected on focus.
- **6.b** Per-cell labels, e.g. "Row 4, cell 7, region 3, outer edge, empty, notes 2 5".
- **6.c** `aria-live="polite"` announcements for conflicts and "Puzzle solved".
- **6.d** Errors marked by shape as well as color.
- **6.e** Selection animation only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

## 7. Retention and habit loop

**What's working:** Per-difficulty completed-today state, view mode, streak on the win modal.

**Issues**
- **7.1 Critical [V]:** Streaks reset to 1 on most days. Last-played is zero-padded (`use-tridoku.ts:185`); "yesterday" isn't (`use-tridoku.ts:282`). They only match on about 73 days a year (`bug-streak.png`).
- **7.2 High [V]:** Win % is always 100% (`use-tridoku.ts:274`).
- **7.3 High [V]:** Solving across midnight credits the next day and locks the real next-day puzzle into view mode (`bug-rollover.png`).
- **7.4 Medium [V]:** No countdown, no "Try Medium next"; no share button in view mode.
- **7.5 Medium [V]:** No reminders or installable app. Streaks live only in localStorage.
- **7.6 Medium [H]:** Per-difficulty streaks mean players who alternate never build one.

**Recommendations**
- **7.a** One `toDateKey` helper; compare with `differenceInCalendarDays`.
- **7.b** Tie every completion to the puzzle's own date.
- **7.c** A global daily streak across difficulties.
- **7.d** Count a game as played on first entry.
- **7.e** Win modal: countdown, "Next: Medium →", time vs best.
- **7.f** Web manifest; then test opt-in reminders.

## 8. Virality and growth

**What's working:** Native share sheet with clipboard fallback; share text includes number, difficulty, streak.

**Issues**
- **8.1 High [V]:** Puzzle number depends on time zone (`lib/tridoku.ts:311-314`). On 6 Nov: LA 09:00 #310, LA 20:00 #311, Tokyo 07:00 #309.
- **8.2 High [V]:** Plain share text (`use-tridoku.ts:576`), no spoiler-free picture, no tracking parameter.
- **8.3 Medium [V]:** `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` 404; nothing targets "triangular sudoku".
- **8.4 Medium [V]:** Square OG image is cropped (see 5.2).
- **8.5 Opportunity:** `/bts` is a strong launch story for HN or Reddit.

**Recommendations**
- **8.a** Number puzzles from the puzzle's date key.
- **8.b** Share grid of 9 triangle tiles plus `?utm_source=share`; reuse as the daily OG image.
- **8.c** `app/robots.ts`, `app/sitemap.ts` and a text "How to play Tridoku" page.

## 9. Monetization

**What's working:** Ko-fi is quiet and loads only when clicked (`kofi-widget.tsx`).

**Issues**
- **9.1 Medium [V]:** Never offered at the win moment.
- **9.2 Low [V]:** Opening Ko-fi isn't tracked.

**Alternatives**
- **9.a** Past-puzzle archive: last 7 days free, full archive paid. Best fit once D7 retention exists.
- **9.b** Printable puzzle packs (PDF).
- **9.c** Ads: don't.
- **9.d** One line in the win modal, "Enjoying Tridoku? Buy me a coffee ☕", at most once a week, tracked.

## 10. Analytics and learning

**Tracked today [V]:** `rules_opened`, `error_check_toggled`, `{diff}_loaded` (also on Reset), `{diff}_completed {time}`, `{diff}_abandoned` (Reset or switch only), `result_shared` (counted before the share sheet resolves). Vercel Analytics also loaded.

**Gaps [V]**
- **10.1** No "started" or "first move" event.
- **10.2** Closing the tab mid-puzzle isn't recorded.
- **10.3** Copy button not tracked (`win-modal.tsx:55`); `result_shared` counts cancels.
- **10.4** Rules depth not tracked.
- **10.5** Ko-fi and Feedback opens not tracked.
- **10.6** Retention can't be measured: no persistent anonymous ID.
- **10.7** Errors, pencil-mark usage and undo/reset counts not recorded.

**Metrics that matter**

| Metric | Definition | Events needed |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | First digit within 3 min | `puzzle_started {diff, ms_since_load, rules_seen}` |
| Completion rate | Completed ÷ started, by difficulty and tier | `tier` on completed |
| D1 / D7 retention | Returning players by first-play cohort | Anonymous ID + `days_since_first` |
| Median solve time | By difficulty and tier | Completed with time, tier, errors, note count |
| Abandonment point | % filled at last activity | `session_end {diff, pct_filled, time}` via `sendBeacon` |
| Share rate | Shares or copies ÷ completions; share-driven visits | Share outcome, copy event, `utm_source=share` |
| Multi-tier days | Players completing 2+ tiers a day | From completed events |
| Rules effect | Completion by whether the last rules step was reached | `rules_step {n}` |

## 11. Feedback loop

**What's working:** Tally is one click away and loads after the page is interactive.

**Issues**
- **11.1 Medium [V]:** No context passed (difficulty, puzzle number, solved, device).
- **11.2 Medium [H]:** Footer only; the best moments are after a win or after a long stall or Reset.

**Recommendations**
- **11.a** Hidden fields, e.g. `data-tally-hidden-fields="puzzle=310&diff=hard&solved=1"`.
- **11.b** One-tap "Too easy / Just right / Too hard" rating in the win modal, sent as an analytics event.
- **11.c** Weekly review: tag responses (bug, rules confusion, difficulty) and tie each tag to a metric.

## 12. Technical quality as it affects the product

**What's working:** Static prerendering, ~0.9 s local load, lazy Ko-fi iframe.

**Issues**
- **12.1 Critical [V]:** The backup puzzle (`lib/puzzle-service.ts:78`) is 82 characters and its first 81 put two 3s on one outer edge: unsolvable (`bug-fallback.png`). If the Action fails for 3+ days every player gets it.
- **12.2 High [V]:** A failed puzzle download fails silently (`use-tridoku.ts:544`, `bug-fetchfail.png`).
- **12.3 High [V]:** No alert if the pipeline fails. Each bot commit to `main` redeploys production (confirmed in Vercel after the review). **[H]** GitHub disables scheduled workflows after 60 days of repo inactivity.
- **12.4 Medium [V]:** Year rollover is untested; the puzzle number keeps counting from 2026.
- **12.5 Medium [V]:** `ignoreBuildErrors: true` (`next.config.mjs:4`), one `tsc` error (`win-modal.tsx:36`); `pnpm lint` fails (no config, ESLint not a dependency); both `yarn.lock` and `pnpm-lock.yaml`; package named `my-project`; `generator: 'v0.app'`.
- **12.6 Medium [V]:** Only 8 of ~57 `components/ui` files used; recharts, react-hook-form, zod, cmdk, embla, vaul, react-day-picker, input-otp, sonner unused (node_modules 473 MB).
- **12.7 Medium [V]:** Each play downloads the whole year (189 KB, ~330 KB by December), including the next 2 days' puzzles.
- **12.8 Low [V]:** `/test` is public and freezes the tab; `/test` and `/bts` aren't excluded from search.

**Recommendations**
- **12.a** Known-good backup puzzle with a unit test; better, bundle recent days and fall back to the most recent.
- **12.b** "Couldn't load today's puzzle · Retry".
- **12.c** Action fails (and emails) when fewer than 3 future days exist; test that every puzzle solves uniquely.
- **12.d** One file per day.
- **12.e** ESLint config, remove `ignoreBuildErrors`, delete unused UI and dependencies, one lockfile.
- **12.f** `notFound()` for `/test` outside development.

## 13. Content strategy

**What's working:** Three puzzles a day with no manual work; 203 consecutive days delivered.

**Issues**
- **13.1 Medium [V]:** No archive despite ~230 past days.
- **13.2 Medium [V]:** No weekly difficulty rhythm.
- **13.3 Low [V]:** #1 would be 1 Jan but content starts 21 Mar.
- **13.4 Missing:** No holiday or themed puzzles; no Expert tier for the Tier 4+ surplus.

**Recommendations**
- **13.a** Schedule by day of week with the tier grader.
- **13.b** `/archive` calendar, 7 days free.
- **13.c** Sunday Expert puzzle from the Tier 4 surplus.
