# Tridoku UI audit

- **Date:** 9–10 Nov 2026 (puzzle #313)
- **Code:** `main` at `a00e80e` (after #56), production build on `localhost:3000`
- **Scope:** every screen, state, component, colour, size and string a player can reach, in light and dark
- **Outputs:**
  - the proposed system: [design system](../design-system.md);
  - the visual comparison: [design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/KCC9s4riFkSrmGv9bWic9M);
  - the rollout: [M5 backlog](../backlog/m5-design-system.md)

**Method:**
- **Screenshots.** 160 screenshots from a Playwright script ([`capture.cjs`](2026-11-09-ui-audit/capture.cjs)):
  - 8 viewports (320×568, 360×640, 390×664, 430×932, 768×1024, 1280×800, 1440×900, 844×390 landscape);
  - light and dark, set through the system preference, plus one theme-toggle check;
  - every state in the handoff list.
- **Code.** Token and component inventory read from the code (grep over `app/` and `components/`) and from computed styles in Chromium.
- **Contrast.** Computed for 60 colour pairs ([`contrast.txt`](2026-11-09-ui-audit/contrast.txt)).
- **Keyboard.** A focus-order walk ([`focus-order.txt`](2026-11-09-ui-audit/focus-order.txt)).
- **Play-through.** `scripts/eval/play-through.cjs` run before any change, as a baseline for the rollout ([`play-through-before.md`](2026-11-09-ui-audit/play-through-before.md)).

**Labels.** **[V]** means verified in the running app or by measurement. **[C]** means read from the code. **[H]** means a hypothesis. Findings already tracked in the backlog link to their item instead of being raised again. Where this audit found new evidence for one, it's in §5.

**Screenshot names** follow `NN-surface-state--viewport-theme.png`, e.g. `08-errors-check-on--i13-dark.png`. Viewports: `se` 320×568, `p360` 360×640, `i13` 390×664, `p430` 430×932, `tab` 768×1024, `d1280`, `d1440`, `land` 844×390.

**Caveat on the win screens.** The scripted solve takes about 2 s, so the times shown (00:02) and the "New Easy best, 03:32 faster" line are real for that run but not typical. That isn't a bug. I checked: the timer counts normally during play.

## 1. Scorecard (before)

Scored 1–10 per area so a re-audit after M5 can show progress.

| Area | Score | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| Visual consistency (colour, type, spacing, radius, icons) | **4** | The warm palette is pleasant, but 60 hard-coded hex values, 21 Tailwind palette colours, 11 font sizes, 9 radii and three meanings for "difficulty colour" pull it apart. |
| Components (one source per pattern, all states) | **3** | Seven button styles, three tab styles, four notice copies, and a hand-rolled menu. Few have designed focus, pressed or disabled states. |
| Layout and responsiveness | **5** | The iPhone and 360 px fit is good (TRI-058), but any tip or notice pushes the pad off screen. 320 px and landscape open scrolled. Desktop and tablet are a phone layout floating in space. |

| Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) | **3** | Board not keyboard-playable or labelled (tracked). New: white-on-teal fails AA, the selection outline and footer fail, 16 targets are under 44 px, and difficulty tabs expose no selected state. |

| Third-party surfaces (Ko-fi, Tally) | **4** | The Ko-fi panel is offset, white in dark mode and can't be themed. The footer buttons have their own style. Tally couldn't load in the sandbox, so I didn't judge it. |
| **Overall** | **43 / 90** | |

## 3. Findings by theme

### A. Layout and fit

<a id="ui-01"></a>
**UI-01 · High · Tips and notices push the number pad off screen** [V]
- **Evidence:**
  - At 390×664 the pad's bottom edge is at 618 px. With the pencil tip, the power-cells tip or the fallback notice showing, it's at **708 px**, and the bottom row (6–9 and Clear) is hidden.
  - Screenshots: `09-notes-pencil-tip--i13-light.png`, `20-power-tip--i13-light.png`, `19-fallback--i13-dark.png`.
  - Code: the notices are inserted between the board and the pad, or above the board, in `components/tridoku-game.tsx:311-319` and `:390-423`, and the board's height formula (`:327`) doesn't know about them.
- **Why it matters:** the first time a player switches to Pencil, half the digits disappear. This silently undoes TRI-058 for exactly the players who are learning.
- **Fix:** one message slot between the tabs and the board, showing one notice at a time. Its height feeds the board formula as `--slot-h`. Add a pad-fit check with a tip showing to `play-through.cjs`. See design system §3.6.

<a id="ui-08"></a>
**UI-08 · Medium · The game opens scrolled down on 320 px phones and in landscape** [V]
- **Evidence:**
  - At 320×568 the difficulty cards are below the fold, so the page is scrolled to tap one. The game then renders with `scrollY` 245 and the header off screen (`06-game-empty--se-light.png`).
  - In landscape (844×390), `scrollY` is 281 and the board is cut off (`06-game-empty--land-light.png`).
  - TRI-058 covers 360×640 and up only.
- **Why it matters:** the first thing an SE or landscape player sees is half a board and no timer.
- **Fix:**
  - scroll to the top when a game starts;
  - for landscape phones (`max-height: 500px`), put the board and pad side by side.
  - Track as part of TRI-096.

<a id="ui-09"></a>
**UI-09 · Medium · Desktop and tablet don't use the space** [V]
- **Evidence:**
  - At 1440×900 the board is capped at 28 rem (448 px). The header text is 17 px, the pad is under the board, and the footer sits far below (`06-game-empty--d1440-light.png`).
  - At 768×1024 the board fills about half the width (`06-game-empty--tab-dark.png`).
- **Why it matters:** desktop players are a real share (R-03). It looks unfinished rather than intentional.
- **Fix:**
  - Let the board grow to 36 rem on tall screens.
  - The canvas also shows a two-column option ≥1024 px: board left, with tabs, Pen/Pencil, a 3×3 pad and a keyboard legend on the right. That's a proposal; the TRI-087 corner controls stay on the board.

<a id="ui-35"></a>
**UI-35 · Low · Footer padding and safe areas** [C]
- **Evidence:**
  - The footer has `pb-24` on phones (`tridoku-game.tsx:482`). That's 96 px of empty space, probably left over from a floating widget.
  - There's no `viewport-fit=cover` or `env(safe-area-inset-*)`. That's fine while the app runs in a browser, but TRI-070 (installable app) will need both.
- **Fix:** `pb-[max(16px,env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]` via a token, decided with TRI-070.

### B. Colour

<a id="ui-02"></a>
**UI-02 · High · Difficulty colour means three things and collides with the rule colours** [V][C]
- **Evidence:**
  - Tabs and welcome cards use Easy `#bfdde2`, which is *exactly* `--tridoku-cell-outer`, Medium `#ecbd6c` (close to inner `#eed496`) and Hard `#e26495` (`components/difficulty-selector.tsx:367-369`, `components/welcome-screen.tsx:34-36`).
  - The view-mode banner is also `#bfdde2` with a `#98ac8b` (overlap green) border (`tridoku-game.tsx:367`).
  - The stats modal uses `text-green-600`, `yellow-600` and `red-600` (`stats-modal.tsx:59-63`).
  - The win modal and share text use 🟢🟡🔴 (`lib/share.ts:11`).
  - So Easy is blue, green or green depending on where you look, and its blue tells new players "outer edge".
- **Why it matters:** TRI-031 made colour mean the rule. The chrome undoes that, and players learn nothing reliable from colour.
- **Fix:** pips (●○○, ●●○, ●●●) on neutral controls. Leave the share emoji until it's tested with Josh. See design system §2.3. **[Decision]**

<a id="ui-04"></a>
**UI-04 · High · Hover is green** [V][C]
- **Evidence:**
  - `--accent` is green `oklch(0.73 0.12 140)`.
  - The shadcn `ghost` and `outline` variants hover to `bg-accent` (`components/ui/button.tsx:248,252`). That covers the header icons, "How to play", "Next: Medium", the rating buttons and the copy button.
  - The green appears nowhere else in the UI except the board's overlap cells.
- **Fix:** a neutral `--hover` overlay, gated on `(hover: hover)`. Retire the green accent. See §2.1.

<a id="ui-05"></a>
**UI-05 · High · Contrast failures outside the board** [V]
- **Evidence** (all from [`contrast.txt`](2026-11-09-ui-audit/contrast.txt)):
  - **White on primary (light): 4.28:1.** This covers Check on (14 px semibold), Share, Undo's note, Retry, "Reset Puzzle", Play and Resume. The large Play button passes at 3:1 for large text; the rest fail.
  - **Selected-cell outline (light), grey rgb(134):** 1.49:1 on green, 2.5–2.8:1 elsewhere. The selection is the main thing a player looks for (WCAG 1.4.11).
  - **Footer text** (`text-muted-foreground/60`): 2.49:1 light and 2.65:1 dark.
  - **Stats "Easy Level"** green-600: 3.02:1. "Medium Level" yellow-600: 2.69:1. Red-600 in dark: 3.76:1.
  - **Inactive Easy tab underline** `#98ac8b`: 2.23:1 against the background.
  - **`/bts` "BEHIND THE SCENES" label** (red-500/80): 2.79:1.
  - Clear-key hover: white on destructive, 3.96:1.
- **Fix:** design system §2.1 and §2.2:
  - accent `#007671` (5.47:1);
  - ink selection (5.6–10.6:1);
  - muted text without opacity (5.55:1);
  - stats labels in text colour;
  - no red hover on Clear.

<a id="ui-06"></a>
**UI-06 · High · Colours are hard-coded in components** [C]
- **Evidence:**
  - 60 distinct hex values in `components/` and `app/`, excluding `/bts`: `#e4e3d3` ×19, `#bfdde2` ×15, `#ccc` ×12, `#991b1b` ×9, `#aaa` ×8, `#1a1a1a` ×7 and so on. Most are in `rule-diagrams.tsx` (59), `difficulty-selector.tsx` (24) and `welcome-screen.tsx` (12).
  - 21 Tailwind palette colours (`text-green-600` ×6, `bg-green-600`, `bg-amber-600`, `text-red-600` …).
  - The board draws with literals: `stroke="#000"`, `fill="#000"`, `#DC2626`, `#222` and `rgba(239,68,68,0.3)` (`tridoku-board.tsx:22,272,305,332`).
  - Dark mode overrides exist only for the region stroke and selection.
  - `globals.css` defines `--cell-*`, `--region-*`, `--chart-*` and `--sidebar-*` tokens that nothing uses.
- **Fix:**
  - board tokens (§2.2);
  - diagrams read the same tokens;
  - delete the unused tokens;
  - the drift guard (§5).

<a id="ui-17"></a>
**UI-17 · Medium · The digit highlight is an off-palette, opaque blue** [V]
- **Evidence:** `--tridoku-highlighted-fill: rgb(59,130,246)` (Tailwind blue-500) fully covers the rule colour (`10-highlight-digit--i13-light.png`). The rules tip draws it with white digits (`rules-modal.tsx:120`); the board uses black.
- **Fix:** an accent tint at 30 % with ink digits, which keeps the rule colour visible at 4.86:1 or better. **[Decision]**

<a id="ui-30"></a>
**UI-30 · Medium · The view-mode banner is a bright slab in dark mode** [V]
- **Evidence:** `bg-[#bfdde2]/60 dark:bg-[#bfdde2]/90`, `#2d5a3a` text, an `Eye` icon and a 2 px green border (`tridoku-game.tsx:367-379`, `16-view-mode--i13-dark.png`). The text is 4.57:1 in dark, which only just passes.
- **Fix:** a `success` Notice with a check icon: "You solved this puzzle today." + Share result.

<a id="ui-34"></a>
**UI-34 · Medium · Dark mode: the board stays light, but its lines don't** [V]
- **Evidence:** the board keeps light fills in dark mode by design, but the region lines switch to `#7e7e7e` (1.66–3.14:1, TRI-068) and the selection to rgb(43). The result is a bright board with faint structure on a dark page (`06-game-empty--i13-dark.png`).
- **Fix:** keep the paper board with near-black lines (§2.2). Offer "dimmed" fills as a **[Decision]** for night play.

### C. Components

<a id="ui-03"></a>
**UI-03 · High · Seven ways to draw a button** [C][V]

| Where | How | Height | Radius |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win modal, Retry, view-mode Share, welcome CTA, rules nav | shadcn `Button` (6 variants) | 32/36/40/44/48/56 | 6 |
| Timer, Undo, Check, ⋯ | `PILL` string in `game-controls.tsx:22` | 36 | full |
| Pen/Pencil | `input-mode-toggle.tsx`, own classes | 32 | 6 |
| Difficulty tabs | `Button` + 10 overrides incl. `hover:bg-foreground/20!` | 36 | top only |
| Number pad | `Button` + `h-14 w-14 rounded-xl hover:scale-105` | 56 | 12 |
| Resume | hand-rolled in `tridoku-board.tsx:200` | 44 | full |
| Footer Ko-fi and Feedback | duplicated class strings in two files | 36 | 6 |
| How-to-play Play | `<Link>` with its own classes | 48 | 6 |
| Rules/Tips | own toggle in `rules-modal.tsx:323` | 28 | 6 |

- **Why it matters:** the controls look related but not designed together. The heights alone don't line up in the corners: the 36 px pills sit beside a 40 px gap rhythm.
- **Fix:** Button (with `shape="pill"`), IconButton and SegmentedControl (§3.1–3.4).

<a id="ui-07"></a>
**UI-07 · Medium · Notices are copy-pasted, and the pencil tip has two bugs** [V][C]
- **Evidence:**
  - Four notices with different markup: `LoadErrorNotice`, fallback, power tip and pencil tip (`tridoku-game.tsx:32-44, 312-318, 391-423`).
  - The pencil tip lacks the `pr-8` the power tip has, so the × sits on top of "to" (`09-notes-pencil-tip--i13-light.png`).
  - The dismiss buttons are 14 px icons in about 18 px targets.
  - The pencil tip says "Press backspace to clear all marks. Press space or 'p' to swap modes" on phones.
- **Fix:**
  - the Notice component with a 44 px dismiss (§3.6);
  - touch copy: "Pencil marks: tap a number to add or remove it.";
  - keyboard keys only on `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.

<a id="ui-10"></a>
**UI-10 · Medium · Dialogs are inconsistent** [V][C]
- **Evidence:**
  - **Title sizes:** 3xl (win), 2xl (stats, rules) and lg (reset).
  - **Close button:** 16 px icon at 70 % opacity with a `rounded-xs` focus ring (`components/ui/dialog.tsx:362`).
  - **Reset confirm:** the destructive action is primary teal, titled "Reset Puzzle?" with "This action cannot be undone." (`game-controls.tsx:196-209`, `13-reset-confirm--i13-light.png`).
  - **Focus isn't returned:** after closing Statistics with Escape, `document.activeElement` is `<body>` [V], because the dialogs are controlled without `DialogTrigger`.
  - **Win modal on touch:** the modal focuses Share on open, so a thick focus ring shows (`15-win-modal--i13-light.png`).
  - **Ko-fi:** the dialog is shifted `left-[calc(50%-1.5rem)]`, with the close button hanging off the corner (`24-kofi--i13-dark.png`).
- **Fix:** design system §3.7.

<a id="ui-11"></a>
**UI-11 · Medium · The menu is hand-rolled** [C]
- **Evidence:** the ⋯ menu (`game-controls.tsx:172-190`) closes on outside tap and Escape. But it doesn't move focus into the menu, has no arrow keys, and doesn't return focus to ⋯. It has one item.
- **Fix:** Radix `DropdownMenu` with the Menu spec (§3.8). Reset in danger text.

<a id="ui-15"></a>
**UI-15 · Medium · Three tab styles, and the difficulty tabs have no selected state for assistive tech** [V][C]
- **Evidence:**
  - Difficulty tabs are Buttons with a coloured bottom border.
  - The stats modal uses Radix Tabs on a muted track.
  - Rules/Tips is a third, custom tablist.
  - The difficulty tabs have no `aria-pressed`, `aria-selected` or `aria-current` [V], so a screen reader hears three identical buttons.
  - The done and in-progress badges are 20 px circles with a 12 px white icon on green-600 or amber-600.
- **Fix:** SegmentedControl/Tabs (§3.4–3.5), Badge (§3.10).

<a id="ui-19"></a>
**UI-19 · Low · The whole pad looks disabled on first view** [V]
- **Evidence:** before a cell is selected, the pad is at 50 % opacity (`06-game-empty--i13-light.png`), and so is Undo. It's the first game screen every player sees.
- **Fix:** keep the keys at full strength with muted digits. A tap with no selection shows "Pick a cell first" in the message slot. Or select the first empty cell on start (to be decided with TRI-062, which proposes the same for keyboard focus).

<a id="ui-20"></a>
**UI-20 · Low · Clear key** [V]
- **Evidence:** an outline key (white in light mode) beside filled keys. On hover it turns solid red with 3.96:1 white text, which reads as "danger" for a routine action.
- **Fix:** an outline key with a neutral hover (§3.9).

<a id="ui-21"></a>
**UI-21 · Low · Pad hover looks like "note on"** [C]
- **Evidence:** keys hover to `bg-primary text-primary-foreground` with `scale-105` (`number-pad.tsx:301`). That's the same look as an active pencil mark (`:303`), so on desktop, hovering in Pencil mode looks like a mark is set. I couldn't reproduce a sticky hover after a tap in Chromium's touch emulation [V]; real iOS may differ [H].
- **Fix:** a neutral hover overlay, pointer devices only.

<a id="ui-18"></a>
**UI-18 · Low · "On" and "just happened" look the same** [V]
- **Evidence:** Check on and Undo's 2-second note ("Removed 2") are both solid teal pills (`11-undo-note--i13-light.png`).
- **Fix:** an `inverse` pill for transient notes (§3.3).

<a id="ui-32"></a>
**UI-32 · Low · Statistics modal** [V]
- **Evidence:**
  - four tiles in one row, so "Current Streak" wraps;
  - an "Easy Level" heading repeats the tab;
  - "✓ Completed Today" and "✗ Not Completed" are title case in green;
  - Best Time is a large teal number while the counts aren't tabular (`17-stats--i13-light.png`).
- **Fix:** Stat tiles in a 2×2 grid on phones, with sentence case and tabular numbers.

<a id="ui-33"></a>
**UI-33 · Low · The win modal is crowded** [V]
- **Evidence:** nine stacked blocks, three of them bordered, in about 590 px. The rating is three loose outline buttons inside a muted box (`15-win-modal--i13-light.png`).
- **Fix:** the hierarchy on the canvas: time, three stat tiles, one primary action, the rating as a segmented control, then the countdown and Ko-fi as quiet text.

### D. Typography, spacing, shape, icons, motion

<a id="ui-12"></a>
**UI-12 · Medium · No type scale** [C]
- **Evidence:**
  - **Sizes:** 11 in use: xs ×23, sm ×46, base ×5, lg ×5, xl ×3, 2xl ×9, 3xl ×4, 4xl ×3, 5xl ×1, `[17px]` ×3, `[15px]` ×1.
  - **Weights:** 5 (including a lone `font-normal`).
  - **Tabular digits** are used on the timer and win time but not on stats, the stats Best Time, the welcome "Continue · 04:12" on cards, or the puzzle number.
  - **Mono:** the timer uses Geist Mono at 17 px; nothing else does except the countdown.
- **Fix:** nine named steps (§2.4). Numbers are always tabular. **[Decision]** Mono timer or not.

<a id="ui-22"></a>
**UI-22 · Low · Icon sizes and strokes vary** [C]
- **Evidence:**
  - sizes 12, 14, 16, 18 (`h-[18px]`), 20 and 24, with stroke widths 2, 2.25 and 2.5;
  - emoji in UI copy ("☕", 🟢);
  - the logo is lucide's generic `Triangle` (TRI-078).
- **Fix:** 16/20/24 with stroke 2 (§2.8).

<a id="ui-23"></a>
**UI-23 · Low · Nine radii, six shadows, ad-hoc z-index** [C]
- **Evidence:**
  - radii: `rounded`, -xs, -sm, -md, -lg, -xl, -2xl, -full and `rounded-b-none`;
  - shadows: `shadow-xs`, -sm, `shadow`, -md, -lg and -2xl;
  - z-index: 10, 30 and 50.
- **Fix:** five radii, four elevations and named layers (§2.6).

<a id="ui-24"></a>
**UI-24 · Low · Spacing has no rhythm** [C]
- **Evidence:** gaps of 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4 and 6, and padding of 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10. The game column alternates `gap-2`, `mb-3` and `space-y-2`.
- **Fix:** a 4 px scale (§2.5).

<a id="ui-26"></a>
**UI-26 · Low · Motion** [C][V]
- **Evidence:**
  - `transition-all` everywhere;
  - the theme icon rotates;
  - the pad keys scale;
  - the selection dash animates under reduced motion [V] (TRI-066).
- **Fix:** named durations and easings, specific transition properties, and reduced-motion rules (§2.7).

<a id="ui-36"></a>
**UI-36 · Low · Three focus styles** [C]
- **Evidence:** `ring-[3px] ring-ring/50` (shadcn), `ring-2 ring-ring` (`PILL`, footer) and `outline-ring/50` (base). They're visible, but each looks different (`22-focus-04--d1440-light.png`).
- **Fix:** one 2 px accent outline with a 2 px offset (§2.1).

### E. Content and voice

<a id="ui-16"></a>
**UI-16 · Medium · Copy drift** [V][C]
- **Case:**
  - "Reset Puzzle?" and "Reset Puzzle" (dialog) vs "Reset puzzle" (menu);
  - "Current Streak", "Max Streak", "Games Won", "Best Time", "Completed Today", "Not Completed" and "Easy Level";
  - rules card titles "Selecting a Cell", "Highlight a Digit" and "Pen & Pencil";
  - vs sentence case on "How to play", "Play today's puzzle" and "Share result".
- **Names:**
  - Check / "error checking" / "Error checking on";
  - pencil mark / "candidate notes" / notes;
  - "Back to game" (`/bts`) vs "Back to the game" (`/how-to-play`);
  - "Games Won" (win modal) vs "Played" and "Win %" (stats).
- **Rule count:** the welcome screen says "4 rules" and `/how-to-play` lists four. The Rules modal shows "Rule 1: Regions", "Rule 2: Edges" (two rules on one card) and "Rule 3: No Touching".
- **Punctuation:**
  - "Loading today's puzzle..." with three dots;
  - "Got it!";
  - em dashes in the tips ("First things first — …");
  - "Tap the Pen / Pencil toggle".
- **Fix:** the content guidelines and glossary (§4).

<a id="ui-25"></a>
**UI-25 · Low · Tips describe the wrong platform** [C]
- **Evidence:** "Selecting a Cell" lists Click, Tap and Arrow keys to everyone. "Highlight a Digit" says "double-tap (or double-click)". The Check tip says "above the board on the right", but Check is beside the board.
- **Fix:** show the input methods the device has. Fix the position wording.

### F. Brand and system pages

<a id="ui-27"></a>
**UI-27 · Medium · Five teals, two fonts and two board styles** [C][V]
- **Teals:**

<a id="ui-28"></a>
**UI-28 · Low · `/bts` is a separate design language** [V][C]
- **Evidence:**
  - **What it is:** "Behind the Scenes: Where AI Struggled", an essay with interactive diagrams and a Presentation mode.
  - **Reachable:** yes, linked from the footer on every screen.
  - **Style:** red mono uppercase eyebrows, 53 hex values and its own button styles.
  - **Presentation mode button:** the label doesn't change when on.
  - **Metadata:** none (it's a client component), so its tab title is "Daily Tridoku" and shares use the game's description.

**UI-31 · Low · Ko-fi and Tally** [V]
- **Evidence:**

  - The footer buttons are a seventh button style.
- **Fix:**
  - centre the Ko-fi dialog and put it on a neutral surface with the Dialog close button;
  - set Tally's form colours to the tokens in its dashboard;
  - footer buttons become `secondary sm`.

<a id="ui-37"></a>
**UI-37 · Low · Rules modal details** [V][C]
- **Evidence:**
  - the step dots sit in an `aria-hidden` wrapper but are focusable buttons (`rules-modal.tsx:357-369`). That's a WCAG 4.1.2 problem: hidden from screen readers but reachable by Tab;
  - the dots are 8 px targets;

  - on 664 px tall screens, "Selecting a Cell" scrolls inside the modal (`05-tips-1--i13-light.png`).
- **Fix:** dots as decoration only (Back and Next already navigate), or a labelled tablist. Show the credit on both tabs.

## 6. What's already good

- The warm paper palette and Geist give it a calm, bookish identity worth keeping.
- The rule colours are consistent on the board, the diagrams and `/how-to-play` (TRI-031 held).
- The corner controls (TRI-087) are tidy and clearly labelled. Undo's "Removed 7" note is a nice touch.
- The share text and OG image are distinctive. Share text is out of scope here, by request.
- Dialogs use Radix, so the focus trap, Escape and scroll lock work (`body` overflow hidden [V]).
- `/how-to-play` is clean, readable and already close to the proposed system.
- `/test` returns 404 in production [V], and dev tools are loaded only outside production (`tridoku-game.tsx:28-29, 473`).
- The play-through baseline matches the M0 review, except the two known M3 gaps.
